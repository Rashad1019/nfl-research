Words used on this page
Moneyline
Bet on who wins the game. Nothing else matters.
Point spread
The favorite has to win by more than the line; the underdog can lose by less than it and still win you the bet.
Total (over/under)
Bet on whether both teams' combined points go over or under a number.
Half bets
Same idea, counting only the points scored in the 1st half (or only the 2nd half).
Sportsbook's chance
What the odds say the chance is, with the sportsbook's cut removed.
Our stats' chance
What this season's scoring numbers alone say.
Our chance
The number we decide on: mostly the sportsbook's chance, nudged a quarter of the way toward our stats.
Need price
The worst odds still worth taking. Anything more expensive is a bad deal even if you think it wins.
